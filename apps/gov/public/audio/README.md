`test-speech.mp3` is Samplelib’s one-minute English speech sample, used as
the player fallback until final recordings arrive. It is not the Babyn Yar narration.

Source: https://samplelib.com/sample-mp3.html
Download: https://samplelib.com/mp3/sample-speech-1m.mp3
The source lists samples as available with no license restrictions.

Final recording URLs belong in `src/components/audio-guide.ts`; they replace
the sample for their respective language and stop.
